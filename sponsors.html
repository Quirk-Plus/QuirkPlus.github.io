Partner with us

The 2027 QUIRK+ Conference represents the largest student-led gathering of undergraduate physics talent in Europe. Anticipating over 1,000 attendees from more than 20 elite universities, this event offers industry leaders an unprecedented, centralised pipeline to the sharpest analytical minds, problem-solvers, and future innovators in the region.

We do not just offer logo placement; QUIRK+ offers direct access to the next generation of quantitative engineers and physicists.

Why partner with us?

Physics graduates are aggressively recruited across sectors because of their rigorous mathematical foundations and complex problem-solving capabilities. By partnering with us, you bypass traditional, fragmented university career fairs and directly engage a highly concentrated, pre-screened pool of top-tier talent.

Our delegates are actively seeking careers in:

  • Quantitative Finance: Algorithm design, risk analysis, and data modelling.
  • Deep Tech & Artificial Intelligence: Machine learning, quantum computing, and software engineering.
  • Aerospace & Advanced Engineering: Fluid dynamics, materials science, and defence.
  • Strategic Consulting: Complex data analysis and logical framework structuring.

The VIP Dinner

The crown jewel of the 2027 conference is our exclusive, invitation-only VIP Dinner, hosted at a premier historic venue in the UK.

Hosting over 100 of our brightest delegates, keynote speakers, and headline sponsors, this evening is designed for high-level, focused networking. In alignment with our commitment to inclusivity and professional standards, this is a fully alcohol-free event, ensuring a sharply focused environment dedicated entirely to career development and meaningful professional discourse.
